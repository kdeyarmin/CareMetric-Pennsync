Using PennSync

As an administrator you are also a clinical user. Part I is the complete PennSync User Manual — the same day-to-day features your whole team relies on.

1

Welcome to PennSync

PennSync by CareMetric is an intelligent, AI-powered documentation and analytics platform built specifically for home health and hospice agencies. It brings your patients, visits, clinical notes, communication, compliance, and learning into one place — so you can spend less time on paperwork and more time on care.

OverviewWhat PennSync is

PennSync combines everyday clinical workflows with AI assistance that helps you write faster, more compliant documentation. Behind the scenes it keeps your notes aligned with Medicare requirements, watches for patients at risk, and keeps your whole team in sync.

AI-assisted documentation

Turn a few observations — typed or spoken — into a complete, skilled, Medicare-compliant note.

One connected record

Patients, visits, vitals, documents, OASIS, and messages all live on a single Patient 360 record.

Built-in compliance

Real-time checks flag documentation gaps before they become audit findings.

Work anywhere

Mobile-friendly screens let you document a visit from a phone or tablet at the point of care.

AudienceWho this manual is for

This guide is for everyone who delivers or supports patient care in PennSync — both clinical and non-clinical (office) team members:

  • Clinical staff — registered nurses and LPNs, social workers, spiritual care providers and chaplains, and therapists (PT / OT / ST).
  • Field & support staff — home health aides and CNAs.
  • Office & administrative staff — schedulers, intake and referral coordinators, records and documentation clerks, billing/office coordinators, and front-office team members.

Wherever a feature is used differently for Home Health versus Hospice, we call it out.

i

A note on roles

Some back-office and administrative screens (Referrals, Documents & E-Signing, Incident Review, Admin Console, Reports & Analytics, Compliance Center) require administrator access. If you do not see a feature in your sidebar, your role may not include it — that is expected, not an error. Office staff who need these tools should ask their facility administrator about access.

Getting the most from itHow to use this manual

  • Use the Contents page to jump straight to a task.
  • Look for “Go to” chips like the one below — they show the exact path through the app.
  • Watch for the callout boxes: Tips save you time, Important notes prevent mistakes, and Best practice boxes reflect how top-performing clinicians work.
  • Figures are simplified, labeled illustrations of the PennSync interface used to walk you through a screen; your live screens show real patient data and full detail.

Getting Started

A quick tour of how to sign in, move around the app, and set yourself up so PennSync works the way you do.

AccessSigning in

  1. Open PennSyncGo to your agency’s PennSync web address in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) on a computer, tablet, or phone.
  2. Enter your credentialsOn the PennSync sign-in screen, enter the work email and password from your welcome invitation (the eye icon shows or hides your password as you type). First time in, you may be asked to set a new password.
  3. Accept the AI responsibility agreement (first sign-in)Before first use, PennSync asks you to acknowledge that AI-generated content is a starting point that you must review, edit, and approve before it is used or submitted. Check all three statements and choose I Agree & Continue — your name, the date, and the agreement version are recorded. You’ll only be asked again if the agreement wording changes.
  4. Land on your DashboardAfter sign-in you arrive on your Dashboard — your personalized home base for the day. Sign-in returns you to the exact page you were headed to, so links from emails and messages land where they should.
★

Add PennSync to your home screen

On a phone or tablet, use your browser’s Add to Home Screen option. PennSync installs like an app, opens full-screen and keeps you signed in.

!

Forgot your password?

Choose Forgot password? next to the password field — enter your email and PennSync sends you a reset link. You can also ask your facility administrator to trigger a reset. Never share your password — every action in PennSync is recorded under your name for HIPAA accountability.

NavigationFinding your way around

Every screen shares the same layout. The illustration below maps the four things you’ll use most to move around PennSync.

  • 1Sidebar — your features, grouped into Overview, Patient Care, Documentation, Communication, Learning & Resources, and Tools. Tap any item to open it.

  • 3Command palette — press Cmd / Ctrl + K to jump to any page or patient by name.
  • 4Notifications — the bell shows new alerts, messages, and approvals.
★

Search beats scrolling

Don’t hunt through the sidebar — press Cmd / Ctrl + K and type “fax”, “oasis”, a patient’s name, or a task. The command palette only ever shows pages you’re allowed to open.

Who can use thisNurseFacility Admin
Go toSidebar▸Patient Care▸OASIS Center

Guided & AI-assistedCompleting an assessment

  1. Open the Assessment tabThe OASIS Center opens on Assessment (Smart OASIS) by default.
  2. Select the patient & visitThe assessment links to a specific visit so it lands on the right episode.
  3. Work through the guided formItems branch intelligently based on your answers. AI pre-fills likely responses from the patient’s history and offers compliance hints as you go.
  4. Review AI suggestionsPennSync highlights clinical pathway suggestions and flags responses that may create compliance or coding risk.
  5. Save as draft or submitSave to finish later, or submit to send the assessment for review and sign-off.
★

Pre-fill OASIS from a note

At the top of the Assessment tab, open Pre-fill OASIS from a Note and paste a recent clinical note. PennSync suggests answers for matching OASIS items, each with a confidence score and the exact sentence from your note it was drawn from. Nothing is written to the assessment until you attest — choose Attest & apply per item, or Attest all ≥85% for the high-confidence set. Only blank items are filled; your existing answers are never overwritten.

!

Always confirm AI answers

AI suggestions accelerate OASIS but never replace your clinical judgment. Review every item — especially those that drive the patient’s functional score and case mix — before submitting.

Documenting a Visit

Documentation is where PennSync saves you the most time. Everything lives in the Clinical Notes hub, where you choose the approach that fits the moment.

Fastest for typingSmart Note (typed)

Smart Note turns a few quick observations into a complete, compliant note in five steps:

1
Select patient

AI personalizes to their diagnoses, meds, and history.

2
Enter vitals

Structured vitals flow to the chart and trends.

3
Write observations

Plain language and bullet points are fine.

4
AI checks & polishes

Compliance is verified; skilled language is added.

5
Review & save

Edit if needed; the visit and audit are created.

  • 1Select the patient — AI uses their record to personalize the note.
  • 2Enter vital signs in the structured form.
  • 3Type your observations in plain language.
  • 4AI flags any compliance issues and rewrites your text into a skilled note.
  • 5Review and Save — the visit and compliance audit are created automatically.

Step by step

  1. Choose Smart NoteIn Clinical Notes, select Smart Note. Always select the patient first.
  2. Capture vitalsEnter vital signs (temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, O₂, pain) in the structured form so they reach the chart and trends.
  3. Write your observationsType rough notes in plain language — bullet points are fine.
  4. Let AI check & polishPennSync flags compliance issues (Home Health or Hospice rules) and rewrites your notes into professional, skilled documentation with medical-necessity and homebound language.
  5. Review, edit & saveConfirm the note reads accurately, make any edits, and save. The visit record and compliance audit are created automatically.
★

Always select the patient first

Selecting the patient lets AI personalize the note using their diagnoses, medications, and history. The more you document for a patient, the better the suggestions get.

Hands-freeVisit Scribe (spoken)

Visit Scribe offers two ways to capture a visit by voice:

ModeHow it works
Record / UploadRecord the visit audio in your browser or upload a recording. Play it back, then transcribe it to text.
Live DictationDictate in real time and watch the transcription appear as you speak; pause, resume, and edit.

Either way, PennSync selects the patient and visit type, captures vitals, transcribes your words, and runs the result through the same compliance review and polishing as a typed Smart Note before saving to the chart.

★

Save 10–15 minutes per visit

Speaking your note in Visit Scribe is often much faster than typing. Narrate the visit naturally — PennSync handles structure and skilled language for you.

Reusable text, instantlyQuick phrases (type “/”)

Quick phrases expand a short trigger into full, Medicare-compliant narrative right inside your note — wound-care language, diabetic education, homebound status, and anything you save yourself.

  1. Trigger the pickerIn the observations box, type / to open the Quick Phrases picker, type a saved .shortcut code (for example .diabeticedu), or tap the Quick Phrase button in the header. Everyday clinical shorthand (120/80, c/o, N/V) never triggers it by accident.
  2. Pick a phraseUse the arrow keys and Enter (or tap) to choose. Badges show each phrase’s scope: Patient (bound to the selected patient), AI (personalized with patient details), or Agency (shared agency-wide).
  3. Review the inserted textThe full expansion is inserted at your cursor. It’s treated as your own draft — it still flows through the same compliance review before you save.

Create and manage your phrases in the Clinical Library (Learning & Resources ▸ Library). When creating a phrase you can bind it to a specific patient — for example, that patient’s exact wound-care orders. A patient-bound phrase appears and expands only while you’re charting that patient, so it can never land in another patient’s note.

★

Quick phrases need no round-trip

The built-in starter phrases expand instantly on the device, so “/” stays fast even on a weak connection.

Your agency’s rules, checked liveFacility documentation requirements

Your administrator can define agency documentation rules — for example, oxygen patients need an SpO₂ reading, diabetic patients need a blood sugar, any wound needs measurements. When the selected patient matches a rule, a Facility Documentation Requirements panel appears in Smart Note showing what this note must include.

  • Each requirement starts amber and flips to a struck-through green check the moment you document it — the panel counts down live (“2 to document” → “All requirements met”).
  • While you’re drafting (Step 1), unmet requirements are a gentle reminder — you can always continue to review.
  • On the final note (Step 2), an unmet critical requirement blocks Save until you either add the missing detail or explicitly acknowledge saving without it. Acknowledged overrides are recorded for your agency’s audit trail.
i

Where the rules come from

Requirements are written by your facility administrator, often from state-survey findings or agency policy. If a requirement seems wrong for a patient, document your clinical reasoning and let your administrator know.

Close the loopVitals & closing a scheduled visit

When you document from a scheduled or overdue visit (for example, via a “Document this visit” link on the patient record or a compliance alert), PennSync loads that visit, pre-selects the patient and visit type, and completes the existing visit on save — so you’re not left with a duplicate and any related alert clears.

i

Vitals travel with the note

Vitals you enter on a note are saved to the visit, so they appear on the chart, in the vitals trend, and in critical-vitals escalation. They reset when you switch patients, so one patient’s readings never land on another’s chart.

Timesheets & Getting Paid

Every staff member submits a timesheet for each pay period. PennSync tracks your hours, visit points, PTO, and mileage, routes the sheet to your approver, and feeds approved timesheets straight into payroll.

Who can use thisNurseFacility Admin
Go toSidebar▸Tools▸Timesheets

The rhythmPay periods, deadlines & paydays

  • Pay periods are biweekly — two full weeks, Sunday through Saturday. The pay-period dropdown lists each scheduled period (e.g. “Jun 14 – Jun 27, 2026”).
  • Timesheets are due before noon (12:00 PM) on the Monday after the period ends. Past the deadline, the form flags the period Past due in red.
  • Payday is normally the Friday after the period ends. If that Friday is a bank holiday, payday moves to the day before (Thursday). Occasionally payroll confirms a different date for a specific period — when that happens, the adjusted date is what the form shows.
i

No math required

When you pick a pay period, the form shows its exact Due date/time and Payday right below the dropdown — no need to memorize the schedule. The payday shown there is the authoritative one, including any payroll-confirmed adjustments.

A few minutes, twice a monthSubmitting your timesheet

  1. Open My TimesheetIn Timesheets, the My Timesheet tab shows a New Timesheet form. Pick your pay period.
  2. Check your service lineA read-only chip shows how you’re paid — Home Health (paid by points + hourly for field staff, or hourly) or Hospice (hourly). Your administrator sets this; if it looks wrong, tell them before submitting.
  3. Choose an entry modeBulk (period total) enters one total for the whole period; Daily entry gives you a row per day with automatic totals.
  4. Enter visits & hoursHome-health field staff paid by points enter visit counts — SOC, ROC, Recert, Routine Visit, Discharge — with each type’s point value shown and a live total. Everyone enters Regular, Overtime, Holiday, and On-Call hours; hospice staff also enter On-Call Visits.
  5. Confirm PTO & reimbursementsApproved Time Off overlapping the period is carried in automatically (a green “+N hrs from approved PTO (auto-added)” badge) — enter only additional PTO hours. Add your miles and any other reimbursement; a standing phone reimbursement is auto-added if your administrator has set one.
  6. SubmitPick your approver (or leave it unset to route to the administrators), add optional notes, then Save draft or Submit for approval. Your approver is notified automatically.

From submitted to paidTracking, edits & approval

The My Timesheets list shows each sheet’s period, totals, and status:

StatusWhat it means
DraftSaved but not submitted — keep editing any time.
SubmittedWaiting for your approver’s review.
ApprovedLocked and included in the payroll export for that period.
RejectedReturned for changes — the reviewer’s note tells you what to fix. Edit and resubmit.
!

Approved timesheets are locked

Once approved, a timesheet can no longer be edited — and you can’t submit a second one for the same pay period. If something changed after approval, ask your approver or administrator to reopen it. You can edit only Draft and Rejected sheets; resubmitting clears the previous review and notifies your approver again.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions clinicians ask most.

How do I document a visit using my voice?

Open Clinical Notes, choose Visit Scribe, and either record/upload audio or use Live Dictation. PennSync transcribes your words and generates a Medicare-compliant note for you to review and save.

Why should I always select a patient in Smart Notes?

Selecting a patient lets AI personalize documentation using their specific diagnoses, medications, and history. The more you document for a patient, the better the AI recommendations become.

How does AI enhance my clinical notes?

It expands your observations into skilled nursing language, adds medical-necessity justification, includes homebound documentation, and checks Medicare compliance — all while preserving your clinical intent.

What if AI generates something incorrect?

Always review AI-generated content. Edit it directly, regenerate, or add your own text. PennSync learns from your corrections over time.

How do I send a fax from my phone?

In Fax, use the camera to capture your document, add the recipient number, and send. PennSync can generate a professional cover page automatically.

Can I create custom documentation templates?

Yes. Use the Clinical Library to create quick phrases that expand into full documentation, including patient-specific variables. You can bind a phrase to one specific patient so it only appears while charting them.

How do I use a quick phrase while writing a note?

Type “/” in the observations box to open the Quick Phrases picker, type a saved “.shortcut” code, or tap the Quick Phrase button. The expanded text is inserted at your cursor and still goes through compliance review before you save.

Why is Save blocked by a “facility documentation requirement”?

Your administrator has defined a critical documentation rule that applies to this patient (for example, an SpO₂ reading for a patient on oxygen). Add the missing detail — the checklist clears automatically — or check the acknowledgment box to save without it; acknowledged overrides are recorded.

When is my timesheet due, and when is payday?

Pay periods are biweekly (Sunday through Saturday). Timesheets are due before noon on the Monday after the period ends, and payday is normally the Friday after the period ends — moved to Thursday when that Friday is a bank holiday, and occasionally adjusted for a specific period when payroll confirms it. The timesheet form shows the authoritative due date and payday for the period you pick.

What is the AI-content responsibility agreement I saw at sign-in?

A one-time acknowledgment (re-shown only if the wording changes) that AI-generated content may contain errors and that you are responsible for reviewing and approving anything you submit. Your acceptance is recorded with your name, the date, and the agreement version.

What does the “AI-generated — verify before clinical use” line mean?

It marks text that AI drafted (patient summaries, insights, incident narratives, and similar), often with a generated-at time. Treat it as a draft: read it, correct it, and only then rely on it or save it.

How do OASIS suggestions work?

AI analyzes patient history, recent notes, and diagnoses to suggest appropriate OASIS responses, highlight compliance risks, and optimize for PDGM case mix. You confirm every answer.

What happens to a flagged compliance issue?

Flagged notes appear where you can review them, apply AI suggestions to fix the issue, and re-save. PennSync tracks your improvement over time.

A feature in this manual isn’t in my sidebar — why?

Some tools are limited to facility administrators. If you don’t see it, your role doesn’t include it. Contact your administrator if you believe you need access.

Glossary

Common terms you’ll see throughout PennSync.

Care scope
Whether you (or a patient) are set to Home Health, Hospice, or Both. It tailors dashboards, templates, and compliance checks.
OASIS / OASIS-E
The standardized CMS assessment (Outcome and Assessment Information Set) completed at key points in a home-health episode. OASIS-E is the current version.
PDGM
Patient-Driven Groupings Model — how Medicare determines home-health payment from diagnoses, timing, and functional status (the “case mix”).
Case mix
The weighting that reflects a patient’s clinical and functional complexity, which drives PDGM reimbursement.
HHA
Home Health Agency.
Homebound
A Medicare eligibility criterion: leaving home requires considerable effort. Documentation must support homebound status for skilled home-health coverage.
Skilled documentation
Notes written in professional clinical language that show medical necessity and the skilled nature of the care provided.
Quick phrase
A saved trigger (opened with “/” or a “.shortcut” code) that expands into full documentation text. A phrase can be bound to one specific patient.
Facility documentation rule
An agency-defined charting requirement (e.g. SpO₂ for oxygen patients) checked live in Smart Note; critical rules must be documented or explicitly acknowledged before saving.
Pay period
The biweekly Sunday-through-Saturday window a timesheet covers. Timesheets are due by noon the following Monday; payday is normally the Friday after the period ends (Thursday when that Friday is a bank holiday), with any payroll-confirmed adjustment shown on the timesheet form.
Visit points
Units of work credited per visit type (SOC, ROC, Recert, Routine, Discharge) for home-health field staff paid by the point. Point values are set by your administrator; no dollar amounts are stored in PennSync.
Smart Note
PennSync’s typed documentation tool that turns brief observations into a complete, compliant note with AI.
Visit Scribe
PennSync’s voice documentation tool that records or dictates a visit and transcribes it into a compliant note.
Teach-back
A method of confirming patient understanding by having them restate instructions in their own words.
Telehealth
A secure video visit with a patient conducted through PennSync.

Administrator FAQ

Answers to the questions facility administrators ask most.

How do I add a new staff member?

Go to Users (or User Setup), send an invitation with their email, name, and role. They set their own password from the invite and are auto-approved on sign-up.

An invitation expired — what now?

Invitations expire after 7 days. Open the pending invitations list and resend it in one click.

How do I change someone’s role or revoke access?

Open the person in Users, change their role between administrator and clinical user, or disable the account to revoke access. Re-enable it any time.

Why can’t I see Telnyx, background jobs, or AI Tools?

Those are platform-administrator (super-admin) tools, not facility-admin tools. Contact your PennSync platform administrator for changes there.

How do I approve a clinician’s license or certification?

Open Personnel File → Approvals, review the uploaded credential, and approve or reject it. Expiration Tracking and Credential Compliance then keep it monitored.

How do I export a report?

In Reports & Analytics, open the relevant tab or the Reports Center and export to PDF or CSV; you can also schedule recurring reports by email.

Where do I update Medicare PDGM rates?

Admin Console → System & Configuration → PDGM Rate Settings. Update the base rate, case-mix weights, thresholds, multipliers, and ICD-10 mappings when CMS publishes new rates.

How do I post an announcement to all staff?

Use the Announcements manager to publish an info, warning, or urgent message with optional scheduling and expiration; it appears on every user’s Dashboard.

How do I create a facility documentation rule?

Administration → Facility Doc Rules. Start from a Quick add preset or New Rule: define who it applies to (diagnosis, medication, wound, care type, or everyone), the requirement text nurses see, the required-in-note keywords (list every synonym), and the severity — only critical rules block saving, and acknowledged overrides are audit-logged.

How do I approve timesheets and run payroll?

Tools → Timesheets. The Approvals tab lists submitted sheets — approve or return each (or Approve all). Then in Payroll Export, pick the pay period and export Home Health and Hospice as separate CSV or PDF files. Only approved timesheets are included; the coverage strip flags anyone who hasn’t submitted.

Why is an employee missing from the payroll export?

Their timesheet isn’t approved for that period — it’s still submitted (approve it), returned (they must resubmit), or never submitted. The coverage strip in Payroll Export names anyone expected who hasn’t submitted.

Can an approved timesheet be changed?

No — approval locks the sheet, and the employee can’t submit a second one for the same period. Return a sheet for changes before approving it; the employee can edit only draft and returned sheets.

How do I set what a visit is worth in points?

Tools → Timesheets → Payroll Setup → Visit Point Values: set the points credited per SOC, ROC, Recert, Routine Visit, and Discharge. Points are units of work — PennSync stores no pay rates or wages.
